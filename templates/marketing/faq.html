Frequently asked questions

Can Kirayas.com manage rent and utility billing together?

Yes. Rent, electricity, internet, water, maintenance, adjustments, and late fees can be included in tenant billing and ledger workflows.

What does the Agreement Builder include?

It supports reusable clauses, parties, guarantors, witnesses, identity documents, e-stamp records, signatures, renewals, and agreement PDFs.

How does WhatsApp AI work?

It can assist with routine requests, approved account information, receipt images, reminders, and escalation to staff when needed.

Can several staff members use the system?

Yes. Roles, groups, permissions, and property-level access control what each staff member may view or change.

How can I ask about a plan?

Use the contact form to prepare a validated inquiry and continue the conversation through WhatsApp.
