From setup to daily operations

How Kirayas.com works

Set up your portfolio once, then manage the rental lifecycle through connected records and workflows.

01

Create your organization

Register the landlord or company account, then configure staff, roles, billing preferences, agreements, late fees, and communication settings.

02

Add properties and units

Enter properties, owners, units, rents, meters, internet packages, photos, documents, and occupancy details.

03

Register tenants

Collect identity, family, reference, employment, emergency contact, and approval information.

04

Build the agreement

Select parties and clauses, attach identity and e-stamp documents, generate the agreement, and preserve lease history.

05

Run monthly billing

Create rent, electricity, water, internet, maintenance, and late-fee charges. Record payments, receipts, and ledgers.

06

Communicate and report

Use WhatsApp AI and staff escalation, track maintenance, review balances, and export reports and documents.
